## Blocking findings

### F-1 · Canvas overlap promise conflicts with its required fallback

**Evidence.** [`53-canvas-renderer.md`](../../../plan/50-frontend/53-canvas-renderer.md#L170) invariant
3 requires that no symbols overlap after layout. Its error-case contract at
[`53-canvas-renderer.md`](../../../plan/50-frontend/53-canvas-renderer.md#L181) instead says that when
layout cannot avoid overlap, it must place with the overlap and log an information diagnostic.

**Why it blocks.** A renderer encountering an unavoidable collision has two incompatible required
outcomes: preserve the invariant or render the overlap. That choice affects both the layout algorithm
and the test oracle, especially for supported 200-declaration scenes.

**Suggested correction.** Choose one explicit policy. Prefer: make the invariant conditional on the
supported, post-collapse layout; require deterministic expansion/reflow until that invariant holds;
and reserve the overlap fallback for an explicitly documented unsupported/degraded scene. Alternatively,
weaken the invariant to require a deterministic, visible diagnostic when overlap remains. Align M3's
sample criterion and the worker golden tests with the chosen policy. Blast radius: `53` primarily;
possibly `05`, `07`, and `62` if the policy creates a supported-scale limit or a new fixture.

## What the plan already specifies

The system does not use a generic force-directed graph. Core supplies deterministic structural hints:
stable component identity, depth-first order, ranks for non-loop branches, flow direction, port sides,
loop membership/orientation, groups, and inferred elements (`25`). The frontend owns world-space
geometry (`D-03`) and then:

1. partitions loop members from trees;
2. puts each loop on a supply/return rectangle in Core-provided orientation;
3. layers tree branches by rank and stacks siblings;
4. attaches those branches to the loop perimeter;
5. compacts/centres the drawing; and
6. creates Manhattan routes: port-normal stub, preferably at most two bends, symbols avoided, and
   route crossings drawn as hops (`53`).

Readability safeguards are also planned: fixed deterministic placement across recompiles, arrows from
solved flow, consistent port-side hints, inferred items visually de-emphasised, label level-of-detail by
zoom, collapsed large groups/scenes, gradients with legends, and an accessible synchronized data table.
The cooling-loop reference circuit provides a worked placement and ASCII render, including the
recirculation path; M3 requires it to be readable with no crossing pipes and no overlapping symbols.
